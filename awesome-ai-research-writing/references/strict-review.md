# Strict Review

Use for reviewer reports, pre-submission checks, and thesis assessment. Review the supplied material against the requested venue or institutional criteria, verifying current criteria when needed. If none are supplied, use transparent scholarly criteria rather than inventing an official rubric.

## Evidence and scope

State what was reviewed: full paper, excerpt, code, supplements, or result tables. For each material finding, give a location or short quote, the problem, its effect on the claim, and an actionable remedy. Separate methodological defects, missing reporting, writing issues, and optional improvements.

Do not mistake an unseen experiment for a missing experiment. Flag “not present in the supplied material” when appropriate. Do not infer misconduct, fabricate references, invent a rating scale, or promise acceptance. Severity follows scientific consequence, not an intentionally harsh persona. Retain real strengths and avoid filling a fixed issue quota.

<a id="reviewer-review"></a>
## 1. Reviewer 风格审查

Assess contribution, claim/evidence alignment, experimental fairness, reproducibility, limitations, and clarity as applicable. Read the actual method and tables before judging the abstract's claims. For quantitative findings, check denominators, baselines, uncertainty, and metric direction.

Default structure, adaptable to the user's form:

1. `Part 1 [The Review Report]`: Summary, Strengths, Weaknesses (Critical), Questions, and Rating/Confidence when justified.
2. `Part 2 [Strategic Advice]`: 问题根源、可救性判断、按影响排序的行动指南。

Use Chinese by default. Tie major weaknesses to evidence and propose the smallest useful analysis, experiment, or rewrite. Prioritize blockers over cosmetic fixes.

Use the provided venue scale for ratings. Without one, give a qualitative readiness assessment. With partial input, give a scoped review and state which overall judgments cannot be made; do not issue a full-paper acceptance verdict from an abstract.

<a id="strict-thesis-review"></a>
## 2. 严格送审评议

Assess `格式规范`, `写作逻辑`, `工作量`, and `创新点或贡献` using available manuscript evidence and the actual institutional requirements. Distinguish visible formatting defects from template requirements that have not been verified. A section excerpt does not establish total workload or thesis readiness.

Use the requested form, or default to:

1. `一、总体评价`
2. `二、分项评议意见`
3. `三、主要问题与修改建议`
4. `四、综合结论`

When the manuscript and criteria support a full assessment, give an explicit recommendation: `同意送审` or `不同意送审`, with the concrete basis and any blockers. When critical material is unavailable, use `材料不足，暂无法判断是否适合送审`, identify the missing material, and still provide all supported local findings. Do not force a binary verdict without evidence. This is an advisory assessment, not an institutional decision.
